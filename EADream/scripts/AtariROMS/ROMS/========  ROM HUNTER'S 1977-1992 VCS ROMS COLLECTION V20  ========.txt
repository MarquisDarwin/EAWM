ROM HUNTER'S 1977-1992 VCS ROMS COLLECTION V20 (December 2025)

Hunted ROMs: 2201

Please know that many ROMs from my V19 ROM Collection have been renamed and that some of them have been replaced by other ROMs!
My advice is to replace your V19 ROM set with this one and to keep your own added ROMs in a seperate map.

The ~ symbol marks the first known to exist version of a released game, or the latest known to exist prototype version of an unreleased game.

Special thanks go to Thomas Jentzsch, who generously helped me examining the code of many of these ROMs.
